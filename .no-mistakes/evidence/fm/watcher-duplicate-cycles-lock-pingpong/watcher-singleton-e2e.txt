SCENARIO 2 - a takeover mid-iteration: the superseded watcher commits nothing
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  Field symptom: a superseded watcher finished its iteration and enqueued /
  absorbed work it no longer owned, so the same parked pane was re-absorbed.

$ bin/fm-watch.sh   # the incumbent watcher for this home
  watcher pid=972168 holds state/.watch.lock (identity published, beacon fresh)

$ fm_supervision_status state/ 300   # is supervision even needed here?
  FM_SUP_IN_FLIGHT=1 FM_SUP_NEEDED=true FM_SUP_WATCHER_FRESH=true
  ✔ supervision IS needed (a task is in flight), so the guard below is a real check

$ bin/fm-turnend-guard.sh   < {"stop_hook_active":false}
  (no output, exit 0)
  ✔ turn-end guard is silent: one live watcher holds this home lock, the turn may end

$ echo 'working: crew turn ended' > state/task.status   # a crew signal lands
  the incumbent is now inside its multi-second signal-grace span

$ echo <successor-pid> > state/.watch.lock/pid   # a takeover lands mid-iteration
  the singleton now names pid 972871; pid 972168 is superseded
  ✔ the superseded watcher stood down (exit 0) instead of finishing its iteration

$ cat state/.wake-queue ; cat state/.watch-triage.log ; ls state/.seen-*
  .wake-queue        : (empty)
  .watch-triage.log  : (empty)
  seen-markers       : (none)
  crew-state probe   : never ran - stood down at the grace gate, ahead of triage
  ✔ no wake was enqueued by the watcher that lost the lock
  ✔ nothing was absorbed by the watcher that lost the lock
  ✔ the crew signal is still UNMARKED - durable for the rightful holder
  ✔ the successor's lock was left untouched

SCENARIO 3 - the work the superseded watcher left behind is still delivered
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  The stood-down watcher marked nothing, so the signal is still pending. This is
  the real operator loop: the successor re-engages, the primary drains, the next
  watcher delivers the crew signal itself.

$ bin/fm-watch.sh   # the rightful holder takes the home over

$ bin/fm-wake-drain.sh   # the primary handles that turn and acknowledges

  ✔ acknowledged (--ack-through 0)

$ bin/fm-watch.sh   # the next watcher of the cycle
  signal: /tmp/fm-singleton-demo.gMKhnd/home-takeover/state/task.status

SCENARIO 4 - the arm refuses to fork a competing watcher during a live renewal
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  forking into its brief unheld window is what compounded the duplicate cycles.

$ cat state/.claude-autoarm-renewal
  pid=975127 successor=stop-renewal
  (arm pid 975127 is alive - the renewal is genuinely in flight)

$ bin/fm-watch-arm.sh   # a fresh arm fires while that renewal is in flight
  arm stdout after 5s of a live renewal : (nothing - it is waiting)
  state/.watch.lock                     : (no watcher forked)
  ✔ the arm did NOT fork a competing watcher into the renewal window
  ✔ the arm is waiting (bounded), not dead and not blind

$ kill <renewal arm pid>   # the renewal clears
  arm stdout: watcher: started pid=980998 (beacon fresh)
  ✔ the arm fell through to a normal start once the renewal cleared - the home is never left blind
